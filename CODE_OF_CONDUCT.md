# Code of Conduct

iamcooked exists to make security less intimidating for people who are new to it. We want contributing
to feel the same way.

## Our standards

- **Be kind to beginners.** "What's a lockfile?" is a great question here. Nobody gets mocked for not
  knowing something.
- **Be precise, not alarmist.** That goes for issues, PRs and product copy: no fear-mongering, no
  shaming people for being "cooked".
- **Assume good intent**, give actionable feedback, and accept it gracefully.
- **No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling or sexualised content** in any project
  space (issues, PRs, discussions, chat).
- **Don't publish exploits against live systems** or other people's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and PRs that break this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do so repeatedly.

Report problems privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/nanaagyei/iamcooked/security/advisories/new) (choose
"Code of Conduct" in the title) or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled
confidentially.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
